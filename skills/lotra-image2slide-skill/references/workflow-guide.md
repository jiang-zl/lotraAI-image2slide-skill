## Construction boundaries

- Keep independently editable semantic units and scientific relations explicit; source-associated prose and inline equations share their native text flow. Never flatten a whole figure into an image.
- Use one suitable preset or one complete path for a continuous contour. Natural parts can form a semantic group. Restore missing structure; preserve layout, aspect ratio and ink spacing.
- Source 3D icons retain projection, volume, natural parts and material. Prefer matched reusable artwork; otherwise use shared face geometry, smooth curves and restrained linear/radial shading and lens reflections. Do not substitute a flat generic icon.
- Blurred illustration edges do not authorize jagged output. Use shared analytic arcs/bands or source-supported smooth native outlines under [semantic curves](semantic-curves.md); retain real corners/openings and existing layer contracts. Scientific curves/data are excluded from automatic smoothing.
- Regular schematic icons use [natural part models](natural-icon-models.md): separate body/windows/signals, measured shape families and fill/stroke, one shared mother with source-specific poses. Dense colour tracing and Bezier continuity do not establish smooth appearance. Review important parts/gaps independently; rebuild a defective contour while retaining satisfactory frame, text, layout and layers.
- SVG may stay an independent vector component; conversion to native parts is optional and must preserve quality. Pure SVG cannot hide bitmap content. HD raster is a proposal until an actual bound local comparison shows substantial important quality gain with no key regressions.
- All cutouts must be genuinely clear at intended reuse size and have plausible complete hidden content. Interpolation/sharpening alone does not satisfy semantic HD. Distinct scientific/photo samples cannot be manufactured by copied/affine/recoloured duplicates; repeated source-supported graphic icons use the checked shared mother route. Never generate scientific measurements, labels, values or relations.
- Profiles stay fixed during comparison. Missing required measures block acceptance; tiny inapplicable SSIM is explicitly marked and its native/content checks remain required. No canned satisfactory coverage, fabricated scores or mandatory redundant retries. Validate changed programs with relevant actual behavior and new-figure counterexamples.
